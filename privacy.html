Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and protection of information across all mobile applications ("the Applications" or "Apps") developed and maintained by Vuttichai Saijaroen ("we", "us", or "our"), including but not limited to Voice2, 1994 Memory, and future software published under our account.

Summary Guarantee: Our apps are designed with strict privacy principles. Your personal files (audio recordings, photos, and videos) are processed locally on your device and are NEVER transmitted, stored, or collected on external servers.

1. Device Permissions & Local Processing

Depending on the specific functionality of each App, access to certain device features may be requested:

  • Microphone & Audio Features (e.g., Voice2): Requested solely to record audio and enable voice functionality within the App. Audio files are saved and managed strictly on your local device storage.
  • Camera & Photo Library (e.g., 1994 Memory): Requested solely to capture photos, record videos, and apply vintage filters or processing effects. All media assets remain local to your device.
  • Local Storage: Used to save user preferences, local databases, and generated files directly on your physical hardware.

2. In-App Purchases (IAP) & Billing Data

If you choose to purchase digital items or features (such as "Remove Video Ads"), transaction processing is handled entirely by Apple App Store (for iOS) or Google Play Store (for Android). We do not collect, process, or store payment card details, banking information, or billing addresses.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To support free features, our Apps may integrate third-party advertising SDKs. These ad networks may collect anonymized system diagnostics and device identifiers to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements compliant with privacy regulations.

Information processed by advertising partners may include:

  • Advertising Identifiers (e.g., IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP Address and approximate coarse location
  • App performance data, crash reports, and diagnostic logs

You can review the privacy policies of our trusted third-party service providers below:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook Ads): Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Unity Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin: AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • ironSource (LevelPlay): ironSource Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Our Applications do not require user account creation or registration. Because all user-generated content (recordings, edited images, settings) is stored locally on your device, deleting data is entirely under your control. You can permanently erase all data simply by deleting individual files within the App or uninstalling the App from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Applications are family-friendly and do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately address the concern.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Updated policies will be published directly on this page with a revised Effective Date.
